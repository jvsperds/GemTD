            New to Gem TD?
            

              Double-click empty tiles to place 5 gems. On touchscreens, tap a tile, then Place gem.
              Pinch or double-tap to zoom the board, and drag to pan. Select one gem to keep as a
              tower; the rest become stones. Make the route longer, combine recipe ingredients, and
              protect the exit.
            

            
Open the Book during a run for recipes and maze guides.
